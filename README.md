# Bean Root

welcome to bean root a simple game about planting plants that protect your 
main bean stalk from mobs!

The game is easy to play!

* Click play
* plant seeds
* take care of your plants and watch them grow
* kill mobs to earn cash
* buy more seeds and plant them
* merge extra beans into the main beanstalk to win the game

## Controlls

* w a s s -> 4 directional movement
* e to interact
* click to interact

## story

You are inspired by the story of the magic bean stalk, and decide to grow your own.
The surrounding bugs do not want to allow that, but luckly you have your plants to destroy them.
To add a complexity later into the game, to win in the game you have to merge some of your grown plants
into the main bean stalk to make it grow taller and reach the skies, that gives the central question of
when is it worth it to merge a plant into the bean, will I be able to survive without it.
